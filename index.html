WEEKLY WORKOUT TRACKER

Goal: Build Pull-Up & Push-Up Strength + Total Body Fitness

Equipment: 16kg KB | 10lb & 20lb DBs | Bands | Pull-Up Bar
PUSH-UP PROGRESSION

Current Stage: High Incline Push-Up

PULL-UP PROGRESSION

Current Stage: Band Pulldowns / Negatives

DAY 1: LOWER BODY & PULL
  • Goblet Squat 3 × 10–12 (16kg KB)
  • Dumbbell RDL 3 × 12 (2x 20lb DB)
  • Kettlebell Swings 3 × 15 (16kg KB)
  • Band Lat Pulldowns 3 × 10–12 (Med Band)
  • Single-Arm Row 3 × 10/side (16kg/20lb)
  • Deadbug 3 × 10/side
DAY 2: UPPER PUSH & CORE
  • Incline Push-Ups 3 × 8–10 (Elevated)
  • DB Shoulder Press 3 × 10 (2x 10lb DB)
  • Floor Chest Press 3 × 12 (2x 20lb DB)
  • Band Pull-Aparts 3 × 15 (Light Band)
  • DB Lateral Raises 3 × 12 (2x 10lb DB)
  • KB Suitcase Carry 3 × 30s walk/side
DAY 3: FULL BODY & HINGE
  • DB Reverse Lunges 3 × 10/side (2x 10lb)
  • Single-Leg RDL 3 × 8–10/side (16kg)
  • Banded Inverted Rows 3 × 10–12 (Med Band)
  • DB Bicep Curls 3 × 12 (2x 10lb DB)
  • Overhead Triceps Ext. 3 × 10–12 (1x 20lb DB)
  • Forearm Plank Hold 3 × 30–45s
DAY 4: CONDITIONING EXPRESS

* Circuit: 3–4 Rounds (Rest 60–90s between rounds)

  • KB Goblet Reverse Lunges 10 reps total (16kg)
  • Slow Eccentric Push-Ups 8–10 reps (3s down)
  • Band Face Pulls 15 reps
  • Kettlebell Halos 10 reps total
  • Hollow Body Hold 30s hold
TECHNIQUE TIPS

Push-Up Secret

Prioritize Incline Push-Ups on a bench/chair over knee push-ups to build rigid plank stability without arching your lower back.

Pull-Up Secret

Focus on Negative Pull-ups (jump to top, 4–5s slow lowering phase) and heavy band pulldowns to build lat strength.
